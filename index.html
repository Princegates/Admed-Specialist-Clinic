About ADMed

Specialist care that puts you first

ADMed Specialist Clinic in Accra exists to make medical care easier to reach and easier to trust. Our name and our promise are the same: an ease to medical care.

Patients tell us the same things again and again: the doctors and nurses are professional and caring, the facilities are spotless, and the treatment is on point. From cardiac and kidney care to orthopedic, gynecological, hematology and urology services, you are met by a team that listens, explains and treats.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and our team will help.

Contact Us
Is weekend dialysis available?

Yes. Dialysis is available on Saturday or Sunday. Please call us to arrange your session.

Where is the clinic?

We are at Worship Curve, Accra, Ghana (Google plus code JVRV+3G). Use the Get Directions button on this page to open the exact location in Google Maps.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page or call us on +233 24 356 0080. Tell us which service you need and we'll confirm a suitable time with you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, any previous medical records, test results or imaging, and a list of medicines you currently take.

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The clinic has a wheelchair-accessible entrance and a wheelchair-accessible parking lot.

What if I have a medical emergency?

For a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for an appointment.
