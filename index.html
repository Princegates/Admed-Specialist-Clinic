FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and our team will help.

Contact Us
How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, or contact the clinic directly. Tell us which specialty you need and we'll confirm a suitable time with you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, any previous medical records, test results or imaging, and a list of medicines you currently take.

Do I need a referral?

You are welcome to book directly. If you have a referral letter or earlier results, bring them — they help your specialist.

Can I bring a family member?

Of course. Many patients like to be accompanied, and we're happy to include family in the conversation with your consent.

What if I have a medical emergency?

For a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for an appointment.
