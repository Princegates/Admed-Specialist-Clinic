Welcome to ADMed Specialist Clinic

Expert Medical Care. Compassionate People. Better Health.

ADMed Specialist Clinic is a healthcare facility located in Accra, Ghana, dedicated to providing quality, patient-centred medical care. We bring together experienced healthcare professionals and a range of specialist services to support your health and well-being under one roof.

Our services cover cardiology, nephrology, orthopaedics, gynaecology, haematology, urology, internal medicine, physician specialist consultations, and medical laboratory investigations. Whether you require a specialist consultation, diagnostic testing, or ongoing medical care, our team is committed to treating every patient with professionalism, respect, and compassion.

Your Health, Our Priority

At ADMed Specialist Clinic, we believe that quality healthcare begins with listening, understanding, and caring for every patient. We strive to provide a welcoming environment, attentive service, and clean, comfortable facilities where patients can feel confident about their care.

Conveniently located in Accra, our clinic is committed to making healthcare more accessible, with a wheelchair-accessible entrance and parking facilities.

An Easier Path to Better Health

Your health deserves expert attention and compassionate care. At ADMed Specialist Clinic, we are here to support you at every step of your healthcare journey.

Meet our founder

A Vision for Better Access to Specialist Healthcare

Daniel Asamoah Appiah is the Founder and Managing Director of ADMed Specialist Clinic, established with a vision to make accessing specialist medical care easier, more responsive and tailored to the individual needs of patients.

With over eight years of experience in the healthcare industry, Daniel has developed an appreciation for the importance of effective healthcare management, dependable service delivery and the role these play in creating a better patient experience.

His journey into healthcare entrepreneurship was driven by a desire to contribute to the development of healthcare services and build an institution that patients and families can trust. In 2023, he established his healthcare business, laying the foundation for what continues to evolve into his broader vision for ADMed Specialist Clinic.

Leadership Guided by Purpose

Daniel believes that a good healthcare institution must combine sound management with compassion, professionalism and respect for every patient. His leadership approach focuses on building a sustainable organisation, supporting qualified healthcare professionals and fostering a culture of accountability and service excellence.

Through ADMed Specialist Clinic, he seeks to help bridge the gap between patients and specialist services by developing a healthcare environment where quality, convenience and dignity remain central to the patient experience.

Looking to the Future

Daniel’s ambition is to establish ADMed Specialist Clinic as a trusted name in specialist healthcare, recognised for its professional standards, responsible service delivery and commitment to patients.

This ambition is captured in the clinic’s guiding tagline: “An Ease to Medical Care.”

For Daniel, these words express the purpose behind the institution: to make the journey towards receiving appropriate medical care simpler and more reassuring for patients and their families.

Building trust. Improving access. Advancing patient care.

Frequently asked questions

For anything else, please call or visit us.

Contact Us
How do I book an appointment?

Use the form on this page or call . Tell us which service you need and we will confirm a time with you.

When are the specialist clinic days?

The nephrology clinic runs on Saturdays at 10:00 AM and the gynaecology clinic on Fridays. Please call us to confirm days for the other specialties.

Is weekend dialysis available?

Yes. Dialysis is available on Saturday or Sunday. Please call us to arrange your session.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. For weekend dialysis sessions, please call us to arrange a time.

Where is the clinic?

We are on Spintex Road, Worship Curve (off Coca Cola Roundabout), Accra (Google plus code JVRV+3G). The Directions button on this page opens the exact location in Google Maps.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, any previous medical records or test results, and a list of the medicines you take.

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The clinic has a wheelchair-accessible entrance and a wheelchair-accessible parking lot.

What if I have a medical emergency?

For a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for an appointment.
