Gynaecology

Know when you need a gynaecologist

Book an Appointment Call us

The gynaecology service at ADMed Specialist Clinic offers private, respectful consultation for women's health concerns. Our gynaecology clinic runs on Fridays.

Many gynaecological problems are easier to treat when they are found early. If something is worrying you, you do not need to wait or feel embarrassed.

Clinic day: Fridays

What we offer

  • Gynaecology consultation
  • Assessment of breast lumps
  • Irregular or heavy periods
  • Abnormal vaginal discharge
  • Pelvic pain
  • Painful intercourse

What is gynaecology?

Gynaecology is the branch of medicine that looks after the female reproductive system. A gynaecologist assesses symptoms, makes a diagnosis, and recommends treatment or further tests.

What to expect at your visit

Your consultation is private. The specialist will ask about your symptoms and your health history, and will explain anything they would like to examine or test before going ahead. You are welcome to ask questions at any point.

When should I book?

Consider booking a consultation if you have:

  • A lump or change in the breast
  • Irregular periods
  • Heavy periods
  • Abnormal discharge
  • Pelvic pain
  • Pain during intercourse

This page gives general information and does not replace advice from a doctor. To discuss your own situation, please book a consultation.
